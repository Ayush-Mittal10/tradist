# NIFTY Quant Lab: High-Level Trading Flow

This document provides a high-level overview of the trading flow one can follow using the NIFTY Quant Lab to systemize trading, remove emotion, and build a consistent edge in the market.

## The Core Philosophy
Trading options successfully isn't just about guessing market direction—it's about mathematical edge, managing drawdowns, and flawless execution. NIFTY Quant Lab is designed to bridge the gap between historical backtesting and live market execution using a **Unified Strategy Engine**.

The golden rule: **Never trade a strategy live that you haven't thoroughly backtested across different market regimes.**

---

## The 4-Phase Trading Flow

### Phase 1: Idea Generation & Backtesting
This is where 80% of the work happens. Using the `🧪 Backtest` tab:
1. **Source Data:** Upload high-quality historical NIFTY option chain data (CSV).
2. **Select Strategy:** Choose from the 6 built-in strategies (e.g., Short Straddle for sideways markets, Bear Call Spread for downward bias).
3. **Configure Constraints:** Set the capital available, lot size, and strategy-specific parameters (e.g., Stop Loss %, Profit Target %).
4. **Execution:** The backtester runs the strategy against the historical data, applying realistic Indian market transaction costs (STT, brokerage, exchange charges).

### Phase 2: Analytics & Optimization
Do not just look at "Net P&L". Analyze the underlying characteristics of the strategy:
1. **Drawdown Tolerance:** If a strategy makes 50% a year but suffers a 40% drawdown along the way, are you psychologically prepared to trade it? (See `03_analytics_and_metrics.md` for a deep dive).
2. **Profit Factor:** Ensure the ratio of Gross Profits to Gross Losses is strictly > 1.5.
3. **Parameter Tuning:** Iterate on parameters like strike width or stop losses. Avoid **overfitting** (finding parameters that only worked perfectly for one specific month but fail elsewhere).

### Phase 3: Validation (Paper Trading)
Once you have a robust configuration, take it to the `📡 Paper Trading` tab.
1. **Connect Broker:** Authenticate with Upstox using your API credentials (or prefilled access token).
2. **Deploy Identical Logic:** The exact same Python class that backtested your strategy will now run on the live NIFTY option chain.
3. **Observe:** Run this for 2-4 weeks. Validate whether real-world bid/ask spreads, slippages, and live market volatility behave similarly to your historical simulations.

### Phase 4: Live Execution
*(Note: As per the current roadmap, the engine supports up to Paper Trading. Live execution requires manually placing the orders generated by the Paper Trader, or a future Phase 4 integration).*
1. **Start Small:** The psychology of real money is entirely different. Trade 1 lot.
2. **Follow the System:** Do not manually override the system during a drawdown. If you trusted the backtest, let the probabilities play out.
